Which groups and businesses the studio shows.

Choose the business groups that appear in the studio, Asset Studio, the asset library and Voice — and the order they appear in. Click a business chip inside a group to hide that business alone. Nothing is deleted: hidden groups stay reachable by link, and a hidden business can be shown again to open its saved assets. Each business also has a client social-crawler switch — only enabled businesses can launch a crawl from Social intelligence.
