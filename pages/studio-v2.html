Fill holes

Rebuild the part of this segment that the segments in front hide

A segmented cut-out is empty wherever another object was painted over it. This rebuilds those pixels from the artwork around them — the same paper and grain — and leaves every visible pixel untouched. Only areas a segment in front covers are rebuilt: a background plate has to sit behind its cut-outs first. The segment's image is replaced; its size, position and animation stay as they are.
