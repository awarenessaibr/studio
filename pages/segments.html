Agent workflow

Use a group in a scene

  1. Find. Search this page or GET /api/segment-library/groups?q=clouds.
  2. Read. Open the group and copy the Agent block, or GET /api/segment-library/groups/<id>.
  3. Insert. Add agent.insert.layers to the composition. Coordinates are percentages, z order is the cut-out order.
  4. Animate. Apply agent.motion. For each layer: x + dx×100, y + dy×100, w×s, h×s, rotation + rot, opacity×a.
  5. Check. Preview here first; a moving group that reads well in isolation still has to sit inside the scene.

Every animation row has a View button. Library animations render their design tracks; motion clips play on a segment group you can change; group motion recipes open a representative group with the recipe selected.
