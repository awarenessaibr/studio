## 1. What "refined" means here

The automatic pipeline captured the raw material; this pass turns it into a
brand system by making four kinds of decision explicit:

1. **Evidence over inference.** Palette, typeface, logo and menu content come
   from the brands' own print PDFs, logo artwork and site copy — not from CSS
   variable frequency or page markup.
2. **One identity per brand, stated once.** Every colour has a name and a role,
   every typeface has a job, every logo has a variant, every dish has a price
   that exists in the printed menu.
3. **No placeholders.** No `Not found`, no `Unknown`, no WP preset accents, no
   palette-strip covers, no "logo" that is a favicon.
4. **Honest gaps.** Where the crawl cannot know something (emails, second-room
   postcodes, licensed font files), the field is omitted and the reason is
   recorded in the guide — rather than filled with a sentinel.

**Rules applied throughout**

| Rule | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Print PDFs are the identity source of record | They are art-directed, current and priced; CSS is theme furniture |
| Palette roles: cream ground, brand-colour ink, gold/red/olive accent | Matches how the menus are actually printed |
| Accent must clear contrast 2.4:1 on the ground | Otherwise the studio silently drops it |
| Families: real print faces only, with a stand-in map | Icon fonts and OS fonts never enter a brand kit |
| Scale ids follow the studio contract (`title`, `heading-*`, `body-*`, `menu--navigation`) | So `fontFor()` resolves instead of falling back |
| Logos: real artwork, print-resolution, background keyed out, named for the classifier | `logo-primary-*`, `logo-ink-*`, `logo-white-*` |
| Photography: brand-affinity checked, classified by filename, no cross-brand files | The pool contains other brands' photos |
| Menus: parsed from `pdftotext -raw`, every price validated against the source text | Price fidelity is non-negotiable |
| Contact: omit rather than placeholder; list second rooms separately | Cards must not print `Not found` |
| Cover: 3:2, 2000 px, a different frame from photo 01 | The gate card is the first impression |

**How colours were extracted.** Each brand's PDF pages were rendered at 90 dpi,
quantised and clustered; chromatic pixels were clustered separately to isolate
accents. That produced the forest green (`#3D504A`), midnight navy (`#17202F`),
olive (`#677356`), ink navy (`#212531`), red (`#BA1F23`) and the three golds
(`#B8A481`, `#B3944D`, `#C7AC62`) now in the palettes. Paper tones were taken
from the page grounds rather than from white pixels.

**How logos were recovered.** La Casa and POMO had no usable logo file. Their
print PDFs were rendered at 600 dpi, the emblem bounding box detected by
luminance, the background keyed to alpha against a global offset (paper texture
ignored), colour unpremultiplied, and variants generated by recolouring alpha:
cream/gold (primary) and ink (dark). Mia's logo was trimmed from its transparent
canvas; Le Bistrot's 2560 px cut was used as-is.

**How menus were parsed.** `pdftotext -raw` (not `-layout`) preserves one dish
per line. A parser extracted name/price pairs, validated every price against the
source text, then a curation manifest fixed names and descriptions by hand
(multi-column PDFs glue description text onto names). Result: **192 priced
dishes and 152 priced drinks**, each traceable to a menu PDF.

## 7. What was automated vs what was judgment

| Step | Automated | Human |
|---|---|---|
| Colour extraction from PDFs | render, quantise, cluster, chromatic separation | choosing which clusters are ground/ink/accent; naming; role assignment |
| Logo recovery | 600 dpi render, bbox detection, alpha keying, variant recolouring | confirming the lockup is the brand's, rejecting campaign graphics |
| Typography | `pdffonts` to list embedded faces | deciding display vs body, weights, scale, stand-in map |
| Photography | dimension/format/dedupe filters | brand-affinity check (other brands' photos sit in the pool), choosing the 9–10 frames |
| Cover | 3:2 crop, 2000 px | choosing a frame that is not photo 01 and reads at gate size |
| Menus | text extraction, name/price parsing, price validation | correcting multi-column name/description gluing, section order and names |
| Contact | cross-source list of NAP variants | resolving conflicts, deciding what is verified vs to confirm |
| Voice | sample extraction | writing attributes, prefer/avoid and the tagline |
| Guides | DOCX generation from the manifest | deciding the table contract and what belongs in facts/print spec |

Roughly **70% of the refined kit is mechanical**; the judgment is concentrated
in brand-affinity (photography), evidence weighting (palette/type) and copy
(voice/tagline). That split is what the improvement plan automates.

## 8. Residual gaps in the refined kit

1. **Licensed font files are not included.** The kit names the real families
   from the PDFs and records stand-ins; exact typesetting needs the venue's font
   licences and an upload.
2. **Mia's logo artwork is 578 px wide** (the only transparent cut on the site).
   Fine at 20–40 mm; a vector or larger cut should be requested for A3.
3. **La Casa's two secondary rooms** have no verified postcode/phone in the
   crawl; the kit lists them as rooms to confirm rather than inventing them.
4. **No email addresses** are published by any of the four brands; the kit omits
   the field and says so in prose.
5. **Booking URLs** are not captured (the sites use on-page booking flows, not a
   visible provider URL); `content.bookingUrl` is therefore absent.
6. **Content schema limit**: lunch, dessert, kids, Christmas and takeaway menus
   cannot be stored in `content/*.json` today; they are notes in the guide.
7. **Print specification is recommended, not supplied.** Stock weights and
   bleed are sensible defaults inferred from the menus, and labelled as
   recommendations in the guide.
8. **Two groups now exist.** The refined pack sits beside the automatic one for
   comparison. Before production use, keep the refined group and delete the
   automatic pack (or merge into it once the studio has a pack-update flow);
   saved compositions keep their embedded kit snapshots, so nothing breaks.
9. **The portfolio card reads "Portfolio brand overview".** The DOCX parser
   hard-codes that title for portfolio documents, so the group name is not shown
   on the portfolio card. Small studio fix, listed in the improvement plan.

## Appendix C — how this was validated

- Palette: rendered PDF pages, quantisation and chromatic clustering; WCAG
  contrast computed for every ground/ink/accent trio.
- Logos: 600 dpi `pdftoppm` renders, luminance bbox detection, alpha keying,
  previews on light and dark grounds.
- Typography: `pdffonts` per menu PDF; families and weights matched to embedded
  faces; scale rendered against the studio's stand-in fonts.
- Menus: `pdftotext -raw`, parser + price validator (0 price mismatches), manual
  section curation.
- Guides: parsed with the real `brand-docx.js`; counts quoted from its output.
- Import: real `createBrandPackImport` + `createBrandTemplateProject`; the test
  pack and its 16 template projects were deleted, then the refined pack was
  imported permanently as `pulpo-hospitality-refined-20261102073203-ae4c`.
